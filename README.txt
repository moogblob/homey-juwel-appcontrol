Bring your JUWEL aquarium into Homey. Turn your HeliaLux on and off, set its brightness, colour and white level, or let it follow the daily schedule from the JUWEL app, and use it in Flows like any other light. Support for the SmartFeed feeder and the EccoFlow pump is included as well, but has not been tested on real devices yet, so keep an eye on your aquarium when you use them.

The app connects through the MyJUWEL cloud with the same account you use in the official JUWEL app, so your devices and Homey need internet access. This is an unofficial community app and is not made or supported by JUWEL Aquarium.
