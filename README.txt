Bring your JUWEL HeliaLux into Homey. Turn the light on and off, set its brightness, colour and white level, and use it in Flows like any other light. When you are done, switch back to the daily schedule from the JUWEL app, or let the lamp return to it by itself after an hour.

The app connects through the MyJUWEL cloud with the same account you use in the official JUWEL app, so your HeliaLux and Homey need internet access. This is an unofficial community app and is not made or supported by JUWEL Aquarium.
